# Milcow user guide

## About Milcow
Milcow is a bulk raw-milk and dairy purchasing platform for business customers (B2B). We sell raw milk, cream, butter, cheese, yogurt and milk powder from partner farms in Korea, by the ton. Our customers are dairy processors, bakeries, café chains and food manufacturers. We do not sell to individual consumers (B2C).

## Sign-up and accounts
Business customers sign up as buyers (BUYER) with their company name and business details. Warehouse staff (STAFF) and administrator (ADMIN) accounts are issued by Milcow. After logging in you can place orders, sign contracts and book pickups.

## How to order
Every product has a minimum order quantity in tons; you cannot order less than that. When ordering you choose the shipping warehouse and how you receive the goods (delivery or warehouse pickup). If the chosen warehouse does not have enough stock, the order is not accepted. The same product cannot appear twice in one order.

## Order statuses
An order goes PENDING (waiting for payment) → PAID → PREPARING → SHIPPING or READY_FOR_PICKUP → COMPLETED. A buyer can cancel only while the order is PENDING or PAID; once preparation starts it can no longer be cancelled.

## Payment
Payments are processed by Toss Payments. The server checks the paid amount against the order total again, so a payment with a different amount is not approved. Only orders waiting for payment can be paid.

## Delivery and warehouse pickup
With DELIVERY we ship to the address you enter. With PICKUP you book a pickup time slot at the warehouse. Each slot has a capacity, so a full slot cannot be booked, and neither can a slot that has already started. After booking you get a gate code starting with MC-; show it at the warehouse gate. A pickup goes SCHEDULED → LOADING → READY → PICKED_UP, and it cannot be cancelled once loading has started.

## Monthly supply contracts
If you need the same amount every month, you can sign a contract. A contract lasts 1 to 24 months, and the monthly quantity (tons) and the price per ton are fixed for the whole term. A contract goes DRAFT → REVIEW → ACTIVE → ENDED. The buyer can submit a draft for review or cancel it before it becomes active; an administrator approves it. While a contract is active, an order is created automatically on each monthly delivery date.

## Contract add-on services
Monthly add-on services can be added to a contract, such as cold-chain delivery, quality test reports and price lock. Their fees are included in the amount of each automatically created monthly order.

## Contact
Leave your question through the Contact page on the website. Anything the AI assistant cannot answer is checked and answered by our staff.
